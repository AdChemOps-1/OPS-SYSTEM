      Load compatibility — what may ride together

      The segregation table from 49 CFR 177.848(d) (read from the eCFR on 10/9/26), which California applies to highway carriers through 13 CCR and the Vehicle Code. X = must not be loaded or stored together on the same vehicle. O = may share a vehicle only if separated so that leakage cannot mix. Blank = no restriction. The app checks every truck's load against this table on the Today list. Admins can correct a cell here; the change is logged. Explosives among themselves and the ammonium nitrate exception are not encoded — AdChem ships neither.

      People

      Access is per company. Someone who works for more than one company is added under each one, and picks the company at the top of the page.

      Add someone

      An address that already has an AdChem account (AdChem Time) gets access at once. A new address is invited: they open this app, choose Create account with that email, and set a password. Drivers: one account per hub — e.g. driver-eb@adchem.pro — signed in on that hub's phones.

      Companies

      Each company has its own customers, orders, BOL numbering, hubs, calendar and sending addresses. Its people see only its rows. Add a company here, switch to it at the top of the page, then set it up on Setup and add its people.
